CASE|Diwali Night at Sharma Haveli|Adithya Sharma|It is Diwali night in Jaipur. Sharma Haveli is full of lights, sweets and loud firecrackers. At 9:15 PM the power goes off for ten long minutes. At 10:30 PM the family finds Adithya Sharma, 62, the owner of Sharma Jewellers, sitting very still at his study desk. A glass of milk is next to him. His iron safe is open and his red account book is gone. Nobody screamed. Nobody saw anything. Five people were inside the haveli tonight, and one of them is lying. The police will not make an arrest until you have followed every lead in the file. The night is almost over. Who did it?
SOLUTION|Abhinav Malhotra, the shop manager, had been stealing gold and money from Sharma Jewellers for two years. Adithya found out from the red account book and told Abhinav that he would call the police the next morning. On Diwali night Abhinav came with a box of sweets and waited for the power cut at 9:15. He slipped into the study, crushed strong sleeping tablets into the milk, and took the red book. The firecrackers hid every sound. All the other suspects were cleared by proof: Rashi was on camera, Neeti was on a video call, Advaith was at his college fest, and Geeta was at the temple.
